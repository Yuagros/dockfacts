## Niche chosen: Mac docking stations and multi-display setups

Justification, in five lines:

1. Apple silicon Macs have model-specific display limits (base vs Pro/Max, Thunderbolt 4 vs 5, DSC), so buyers search exact configurations rather than "best dock".
2. A dock is a $150–$400 purchase; even at 2–4% affiliate rates, one sale matters at low traffic, and the cookie window is generous.
3. Head terms are owned by Macworld and Wirecutter, but "dual 4K 144Hz dock for M5 Air" and "TS5 vs TS5 Plus" are thin and answerable from primary specs.
4. Apple and vendor spec pages document every claim, so an AI research desk can be accurate and transparent instead of inventing hands-on reviews.
5. New Macs ship yearly, the compatibility questions repeat, and a display-limits cheat sheet is a free lead magnet that needs no paid account.
