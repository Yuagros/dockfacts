DockFacts is a static site. It has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter, and — at launch — no analytics. This page describes what that means in practice.

## What we collect directly

**Nothing.** There is no sign-up form, no contact form, no cookie set by DockFacts, and no first-party tracking script. We do not build profiles of readers.

If that changes — for example, if a privacy-friendly analytics service is added — this page will be updated before the change goes live, and the analytics will be disclosed here.

## Hosting logs

The site is served by a static hosting provider (GitHub Pages, or Cloudflare Pages if the deployment changes). Like any web server, the provider may log requests, including IP addresses, user agents, and timestamps, for security and operational purposes. Those logs are controlled by the provider, not by DockFacts. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for the current hosting arrangement.

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, you leave DockFacts and arrive at a merchant such as Amazon. The merchant or its affiliate network may set a cookie to attribute the sale. That cookie is set by them, on their site, under their privacy policy. DockFacts does not receive your name, address, payment details, or order contents from the merchant — only, if a sale is attributed, an aggregated commission report.

## External links

Guides link to manufacturer specification pages, standards bodies, and Apple documentation. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Clicking an external link is a request to that site, which will see your IP address and request headers as any web server would.

## Children

This site is intended for adults making purchasing decisions. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because DockFacts does not collect personal data directly, there is no first-party profile to access, correct, or delete. If you believe the hosting provider's logs contain your data and you want to exercise a rights request, contact the provider. If you believe DockFacts itself holds data about you, use the repository issue tracker (linked in the footer once the site is live) and we will investigate.

## Changes

If the site adds analytics, a newsletter, or any other data collection, this page will be updated first and the effective date below will change. We will not quietly add tracking behind a page that says we do not track.

## Contact

There is no contact email published at launch. The repository issue tracker is the monitored channel once the site is live.
