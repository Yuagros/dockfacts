## Topic rules

- No article that requires hands-on testing to be useful. If the answer depends on measuring latency, heat, or build quality, do not publish it.
- No article about a product we cannot verify on the vendor's own page.
- No "best X" article without a stated configuration and a dated source for every pick.
- At most one commercial article per day; pair it with an explainer or reference page where possible.
- If a topic turns out to be unverifiable, mark it `blocked` and replace it rather than guessing.
