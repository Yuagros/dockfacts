Affiliate disclosure

How DockFacts makes money, which links are affiliate links, and the rules that keep commissions out of our recommendations.

Updated 8 November 2026

DockFacts is a reader-supported site. This page explains exactly what that means, in plain language, because a disclosure you need a law degree to understand is not a disclosure.

The short version

Some links to products on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the retailer may pay DockFacts a commission. The price you pay is the same. We do not add a fee, and we do not get a better price for you.

We only recommend products we would include if no commission existed. The commission rate is not a ranking factor.

Current status

No affiliate program is active on this site yet, so every product link is a plain merchant link and DockFacts earns nothing from it.

Which programs

DockFacts participates, or intends to participate, in affiliate programs for the hardware it covers. The first is the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When a link belongs to an active affiliate program, it is marked in the page source with rel="sponsored nofollow" and the page carries a disclosure near the top. If no program is active, the link is a plain merchant link and DockFacts earns nothing from it. This site is new; assume nothing is monetised until the inline note on the page says otherwise.

We do not publish an affiliate ID, coupon code, or tracking parameter that we have not been issued. If you see a link without a disclosure, it is not an affiliate link.

What commissions do not buy

  • A placement. Brands cannot pay to be included, reordered, or called "best".
  • A verdict. No brand sees a draft, approves a quote, or gets a negative finding removed.
  • A rating. We do not publish star ratings or "editor's scores", so there is nothing to buy.
  • A price claim. We do not call a product cheap, discounted, or good value unless we can link to a dated source.

Prices and availability

Prices, stock, and shipping are set by the merchant, not by DockFacts. A price shown on a merchant page can change between your click and your checkout. We link to the merchant so you can see the current number rather than a snapshot that may be months old.

Cookies and tracking

DockFacts does not set advertising cookies. When you click an affiliate link, the merchant or its affiliate network may set a cookie on its own site to attribute the sale. That happens after you leave DockFacts and is governed by the merchant's privacy policy. Our privacy page explains what this site itself collects — which, at launch, is nothing.

Why we disclose

This disclosure is intended to comply with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), and with the disclosure requirements of the affiliate programs we join. We keep it visible on every article, not buried in a footer.

If a program's terms require a specific wording or placement, those terms win. We do not join a program whose terms we cannot meet.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or if you believe a link is mislabelled, the repository issue tracker (linked in the footer once the site is live) is the right place to raise it.
