# Correction log

Every factual change to a published guide gets a row here. The article's `updated` date must change with it.

| Date | Page | What changed | Source | Reported by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | — | No corrections yet. | — | — |

## Process

1. Verify the correction against a primary source.
2. Edit the article; update `updated` in the front matter.
3. Add a row above.
4. `npm run build`.
5. Commit with `Fix: <page> — <what changed>`.

If a product is discontinued, remove it from the guide and from `affiliate_ids.json`; do not leave a dead link. If a claim cannot be re-verified, soften it to what the source actually says.
